Course Objectives

  1. To acquaint students with the basic concepts of Direct Taxes and Income Tax.
  2. To acquaint students with computation of Income from Salaries.
  3. To acquaint students with computation of Income from House Property.
  4. To acquaint students with computation of Income from Business/Profession.

Course Outcomes

  1. CO1: Recall and explain key tax terms and concepts under the Income Tax Act.
  2. CO2: Apply provisions of exemptions, deductions and tax computation to real-life cases.
  3. CO3: Analyse differences in treatment of income from salary, house property and business.
  4. CO4: Evaluate tax-planning strategies for individuals based on income sources and exemptions.
  5. CO5: Create complete tax files and computation sheets for various taxpayers.
